# Final check before you hand it over

Run through this before you send the Open in Editor link. Fix whatever fails — calling `create_video_edit` again is free.

**Story**
- [ ] Something interesting is on screen in the first second, and the main idea is readable within two seconds.
- [ ] Every shot has a job. Nothing is there only because the user once mentioned it.
- [ ] It ends on purpose: one call to action or a clear final moment, held for 1.5–2 s.

**Picture**
- [ ] Every clip is in the final frame shape, or shown whole on purpose (`fit` "contain" with the blurred background).
- [ ] The same person or product looks the same in every shot.
- [ ] No broken AI moments left in: melting hands, changing faces, the frozen first frame, the drifting last second.

**Pacing**
- [ ] Shot lengths vary and suit the energy; the best shot comes near the end.
- [ ] Cuts fall on movement or on the beat, and directions match across the cuts.
- [ ] One or two transition styles, kept short.

**Text**
- [ ] Short lines, on screen long enough to read (about 0.4 s a word plus 0.5 s).
- [ ] Inside the phone safe zone, not covering the face or the product.
- [ ] One accent colour; the same style and animation throughout.

**Sound**
- [ ] The music fits the mood, doesn't stop early and fades out at the end.
- [ ] Music sits under the voice (0.15–0.3); the clips are muted where their own sound would clash.

**Hand-over**
- [ ] Say what you made in two or three lines: the length, the shape, the idea.
- [ ] Name what they may want to adjust in the editor (a caption's timing, where the music starts).
- [ ] Mention the export price from the result.
- [ ] Offer one natural next step: a 9:16 version, a shorter cut, a version without text.
